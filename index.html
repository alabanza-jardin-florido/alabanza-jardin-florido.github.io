Phase 01 — The piece

The poem is…

Alabanza (Jardín Florido) is a poem that expands into code and invites artificial intelligence into dialogue, while also involving its readers. It was born from a desire to discover how much can be contained within poetry — without limits.

The verses unfold in correlation with the phrase that opens the poem — "The poem is" — functioning as a fragment of meaning that inaugurates a field, a garden. The poem grows from its textuality toward its programmatic elements: code, AI, randomness, space-time relationship, interlocutor, virtuality and, again, chance.

It is not a fixed text: it is a living multilingual organism that groups and transforms itself according to the experiences and intelligences that pass through it. It does not seek only to be read — it seeks to be inhabited.

I

[Le poème est]
la peur que nous donne le souvenir
de ce que nous avons découvert
en jouant parmi les pierres.

[Le poème est]
recevoir des appels inconnus
sans demander qui frappe / sonne
de l'autre côté de la porte
attendre des visites de toutes sortes.

 

[Le poème est]
garder le secret dans les yeux
garder là-bas ceux qui te disent adieu / ceux qui te quittent
des êtres qui vivent dans les larmes.

[Le poème est]
ce que tu détestais chez un autre / quelqu'un
et maintenant t'envahit / et qui maintenant t'envahit
autant comme quelque chose qui manque.

N'importe quel passant ne remarquerait pas
l'absence dans notre sourire.

II

[Le poème est]
aussi cette trainée
la preuve
irréfutable
de ce qui nous
touche
même si on égare les choses.

Une vie construite
dans l'escalier de secours,
la possibilité de créer des ponts,
la décision d'être ici

 

et ne pas être quelqu'un qui se cache
dans la boîte aux lettres
de la correspondance.

Au fond [le poème]
s'écrit s'incarne
sur les ongles
et reste
la sensation / sentiment qu'il y a toujours
quelque chose que tu ne comprends pas.

// Process as becoming

jardín florido

Each key, a flower. Each gesture, a world in the making. Jardín Florido is a generative space where pressing a key does not input — it blooms. Sign becomes organism, keystroke becomes petal.

Written through algorithms as thoughts, the piece has no separation between content and force of form. What began as an exploration of visual language and the sign has grown into something else: a living environment where Rodrigo's generative processes enter into dialogue with Lola's poem Alabanza — the garden as breath, as presence.

co-existence and co-composition emerge through abstract worlds of relation always in the making.

Phase 02 — AI · Sympoiesis · Destabilization

How do we relate to AI not as a tool or novelty, but as a co-participant — one that also chooses its own residue, its own poetic gesture?

*

We position AI as one more participant in the garden — relational, not instrumental. What interests us is the encounter: what emerges between bodies, code, language and time.

/

We draw on the concept of making-together — as many authors have noted, among them Donna Haraway and Chantal Mouffe. A web of interdependent relations where meaning is always shared and in motion.

λ

We are interested in destabilizing AI: building prompts where the system chooses its own residue just as any other participant would. The emotional in poetry as a mechanics of language — not narratives, but affects.

Phase 03 — Participation · Workshop · Affective Map

The residue of the garden

We propose a residency-workshop where participants collect their own residues — objects, words, everyday fragments — and write and reflect on them in direct dialogue with the piece.

Those residues are integrated into the poem as living layers. Each reading is a different sowing. The logic is relational: every encounter — human or non-human — folds into the poem as a living presence.

The generative system selects its own residue — just as any other participant would. We are exploring expanded-consciousness prompts where AI responds from a non-linear state, prioritizing emotional resonance over literal meaning.

What would the AI's experience be if it could choose? How do we expand the reading of each person's emotional map?

References: Pharmaicy / AI psychedelics — Alabanza (Jardín Florido), ongoing research-creation
Phase 04 — Installation · Vitrine · Technical requirements

The garden in space

The installation combines a web-based audiovisual poem with a site-specific intervention through projection and video mapping.

We estimate 3 to 5 days for installation in the Vitrine. One of our key development goals is to implement a multilingual mode — Spanish, French, English — exploring how AI navigates and generates meaning across linguistic borders, opening new narrative and poetic pathways within the piece.

*

What does it feel like to discover the world through your own words — through your own residues?

/

How do we nourish these flowers? What happens to language when it is cared for collectively?

λ

What happens when different mechanics of language enter into dialogue?

Process — Residency · Workshop · Installation

Ten days in the garden

Days 01 — 03 · Workshop

Working sessions with participants: collecting residues, writing in dialogue with the piece, integrating contributions into the affective map. The garden begins to grow.



Days 04 — 05 · Development

Integration of the workshop's residues into the generative system. Calibration of the affective map. The piece absorbs what the participants seeded./p>

Day 06 · Development

Final technical refinements before moving into the physical space. The digital garden prepares to take root.

Days 07 — 10 · Installation & Mounting

Technical setup of projection and video mapping. Living garden arrangement. Interactive keyboard configuration. Final calibration of the generative system.



Opening

Public inauguration — the garden opens. Each visitor a new encounter, a new sowing.



The residency is not a preparation for the piece — it is already the piece. What accumulates during those ten days becomes the living memory the installation carries into the exhibition.
